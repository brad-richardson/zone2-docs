The easy-day specialist

The Zone 2 ride that stays easy.

Power changes so your effort doesn’t. Zone2 watches your heart rate and quietly eases your smart trainer to keep the ride in your easy aerobic band — so you finish the ride you intended, not one you accidentally raced.

What you get

Stays easy

Effort, not watts

Pick a band and pedal. Zone2 nudges the trainer every few seconds to keep your heart rate in the easy aerobic zone — and backs off faster than it pushes.

Safe by design

It fails toward easy

A hard heart-rate ceiling, a power limit you set, and a lost-signal rule that eases the trainer down — never up. The safety logic lives in a tested core, not the UI.

Yours

Private & local

No account, no servers, no tracking. Your rides stay on your device. Write to Apple Health if you want; export a standard FIT file any time.

Honest

Measured, not guessed

Set your band from age, threshold, or a short DFA‑a1 step test — with its uncertainty shown plainly, never dressed up as more precise than it is.

For experienced cyclists Stop turning recovery and endurance rides into tempo. Zone2 backs off ERG power automatically when cardiac drift arrives, so the easy day remains the easy day — no watching HR and nudging the trainer down yourself.

Built in the open

Zone2's decision-making — the controller, the calibration, the safety invariants — lives in a portable, I/O-free core that the same tests exercise from Rust, Python, and Swift. I wrote up how it works, what the research says, and what I got wrong along the way, plus a small set of real (scrubbed) ride data so you don’t have to start from nothing like I did.
