The best way to get help or report a problem is to open an issue on GitHub. Issues are read directly by the developer.

Get help & report bugs

Open a new issue →  ·  Browse existing issues

Helpful things to include

  • What you were doing and what happened (vs. what you expected).
  • Your device and iOS version, and the app version shown at the bottom of Settings.
  • Your trainer and heart-rate strap models.
  • If it’s a connection issue, the diagnostics bundle is gold: Settings → Diagnostics → Export.

Common questions

Which trainers and straps work? Zone2 needs an FTMS smart trainer with Bluetooth ERG target-power control and a direct-Bluetooth heart-rate chest strap. It is developed and validated on a Wahoo KICKR and Polar H10; other compatible devices are not individually tested. Apple Watch and wrist sensors are not supported.

How does it keep my ride easy? It uses your saved heart-rate band to adjust the trainer’s power as you respond — see how it works.

Is my data private? Yes — see the Privacy Policy. Zone2 has no servers and collects nothing; your data stays on your device.
